    Manufacturing defect model

    This model predicts whether a manufactured part is likely to be defective from five machine-sensor readings. Pick a scenario or move a slider, and the model running on this server answers instantly. It's trained on synthetic data by design, so it shows serving and honest evaluation, not real-factory performance.

    What it can't tell you

    Useful here: the page shows the inputs, the probabilities and what drives them, so the prediction isn't a black box.

    Not a deployment claim: synthetic data can't establish field performance. A real deployment would need representative labels, calibration, a chosen threshold, drift monitoring and human review.
